Molecular Sieves & Adsorbent Technologies

Synthetic zeolites and activated aluminas engineered for selective adsorption based on molecular size, polarity, and pore geometry.

Process Physics

Primary Industrial Applications

Molecular sieves provide high-capacity dehydration and contaminant removal for critical process streams where water slip leads to freezing or catalyst poisoning.

Target Applications

  • • Natural Gas Dehydration: Moisture removal to < 0.1 ppmv upstream of cryogenic recovery.
  • • Liquid Hydrocarbon Drying: Dehydration of propane, butane, and alkylation feedstocks.
  • • Hydrogen Purification: Pressure Swing Adsorption (PSA) polishing beds.
  • • Contaminant Control: Co-adsorption control for trace H2S, CO2, and mercaptans.
Troubleshooting & Selection

Process Challenges Solved

Proper selection prevents common operating failures in adsorption beds through precise pore sizing and binder strength selection.

Process Failure Mode Root Cause Engineering Selection Resolution
Hydrate freezing in cryogenic exchangers Water slip from bed breakthrough Select high-kinetic 3A or 4A synthetic zeolites with optimized mass transfer zones.
Bed coking & hydrothermal degradation Improper thermal regeneration cycles Specify thermal-shock resistant binders and custom regeneration temperature profiles.
Capacity loss / rapid pressure drop Liquid hydrocarbon co-adsorption Utilize size-selective pore openings (3A) to exclude heavier hydrocarbons.
