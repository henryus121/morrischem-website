Troubleshooting & Selection

Process Challenges Solved

Addressing catalyst deactivate mechanisms through specialized bed loading strategies and high-stability substrates.

Process Failure Mode Root Cause Engineering Selection Resolution
Rapid pressure drop across reactor bed Particulate deposition & crust formation on top bed Install graded voidage ceramic topping media and active trap catalysts.
Premature activity loss / poisoning Silica, arsenic, or heavy metal contaminant migration Incorporate high-surface-area, high-capacity guard catalysts upstream.
Hot-spot formation & maldistribution Non-uniform fluid flow and poor bed density packing Specify dense-loading methodologies and uniform extrudate geometry.
