Industrial Water Treatment Chemistries

Specialized chemical formulations engineered for scale inhibition, corrosion mitigation, and bio-fouling control in heavy utility circuits.

Process Physics

Primary Industrial Applications

Industrial cooling loops and boiler systems require tailored chemical regimes to prevent thermal efficiency losses, unscheduled downtime, and premature equipment replacement.

Target Applications

  • • Cooling Water Circuits: High-efficiency scale and corrosion inhibitors for recirculating towers.
  • • Boiler Feedwater Treatment: Oxygen scavengers, alkalinity builders, and condensate line protection.
  • • Membrane Protection: Reverse Osmosis (RO) antiscalants and bio-dispersants.
  • • Effluent Clarification: High-molecular-weight coagulants and flocculants for industrial wastewater.
